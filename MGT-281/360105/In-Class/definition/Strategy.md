## Lecture's topics

Strategy is the way in which an organisation seeks to achieve its goals . It is the direction and scope of an organisation over the long term, which achieves advantage in a changing environment through its configuration of resources and competences with the aim of fulfilling stakeholder expectations.

### The Four Grand Strategies
- **Growth strategy**: This involves expanding the organisation's activities. 
- **Stability strategy**: This involves maintaining the organisation's current activities. 
- **Retrenchment strategy**: This involves reducing the organisation's activities. 
- **Combination strategy**: This involves a combination of the above strategies. 

### Porter's Competitive Strategies

- **Cost leadership**: This involves being the lowest-cost producer in the industry. 
	> Note: a low cost base will not in itself bring competitive advantage.  Consumers must see the product as being worth the price. 
- **Differentiation**: This involves offering a unique product or service. 
- **Focus**: This involves targeting a specific market segment.
