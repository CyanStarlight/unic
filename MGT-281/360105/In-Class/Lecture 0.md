---

## The basic function of Management
- Planning
- Organising
- Leading
- Controlling
>The **aim of this course** is to introduce students to the basic concepts , functions and  tools of management. 

Teaching and learning methods :
- Lectures (short and interactive))
- Group activities (work in class in small groups)
- Homework (studying outside the class)

Any work that you do outside the class is up to you, but I expect you to take notes, participate and study the material. 

>In case you are interested in the book on management:
-	*Management* by Stephen P. Robbins and Mary Coulter
