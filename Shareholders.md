# Types of Shares
## Ordinary Shares
- Ordinary shares are the most common type of shares issued by a company. They are also known as **equity shares**.  
	Ordinary shareholders have the right to vote at the company's annual general meeting (AGM) and to receive dividends.  
	They are the last to be paid if the company goes into liquidation. 
	- Ordinary shares are considered to be **high-risk** investments because their value can fluctuate depending on the company's performance. 
	- However, they also have the potential for **high returns** if the company does well. 
	- Ordinary shareholders have the right to receive dividends, but only after any preferred shareholders have been paid. 
- Some companies issue different classes of ordinary shares, which can have different voting rights or dividend entitlements. 
	- For example, some companies have **A shares** and **B shares**, with A shares having more voting rights than B shares. 
	- This can be used to give certain shareholders more control over the company.
