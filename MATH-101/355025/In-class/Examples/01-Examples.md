## Example 1
1. $A = \{x : x \in \mathbb{Z} \text{ and } x^2 + x 4x = 12\}$.
    - $x^2 + 4x = 12 \rightarrow x^2 + 4x - 12 = 0$
    - $\rightarrow (x + 6)(x - 2) = 0$
    - $\rightarrow x = -6, 2$
  - $A = \{-6, 2\}$
  - $\text if\ A = \{x : x \in \mathbb{Z} x^2 + 4x = 12\} = \{2\}$
